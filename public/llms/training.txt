# LuxOps — Hotel training and operational support

> Tailored training, operational advisory and hotel technology adoption for hotel teams, department heads and management.

The approach combines people and team development, innovation in working methods, and lasting operational references (SOPs, checklists and service standards). Training is adapted to the property and delivered on site or remotely, in French and English. Department training is a service discussed with LuxOps; purchasing a digital manual does not book a training programme.
